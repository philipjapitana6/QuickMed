How to Install QuickMed on Android

  1. Tap the Download QuickMed APK button at the top of this page.
  2. Open your phone's Downloads folder or tap the completed download notification.
  3. If Android prompts for permission, tap Settings and enable Allow from this source.
  4. Tap Install and launch QuickMed from your home screen.
Why does Android show a security prompt?

Because QuickMed is installed directly from our official release page rather than the Google Play Store, Android asks for confirmation before installing the APK.

How do I update QuickMed later?

Revisit this official website anytime to download the newest release APK and install it directly over your existing version.
